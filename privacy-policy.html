TV Remote – Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 3, 2026

Summary: TV Remote is designed to control compatible TVs and other supported devices over the user's local network. The app does not require an account and is designed to operate without a cloud service or remote-control backend.

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how the TV Remote Android application ("TV Remote", "the App", "we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use the App.

2. Information We Collect

TV Remote does not require you to create an account and does not intentionally collect personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, contacts, or location.

The App does not intentionally sell personal information or use your information for advertising or behavioral profiling.

3. Local Network Communication

TV Remote communicates with compatible TVs and supported devices on your local network so that you can discover, pair with, and control them. Depending on your Android version and device, Android may ask you to grant permission for local-network access.

Remote-control commands and device communication are intended to occur directly between your phone and the selected device. The App does not require a cloud relay server for normal remote-control operation.

4. Pairing and Security Information

When you pair the App with a supported TV, the App creates and uses a device identity for secure communication. The App uses Android Keystore protection for the client private key.

The private key is intended to remain protected on the user's Android device and is not intentionally uploaded to an external server by the App.

For authenticated reconnection, the App may retain information required to recognize the paired device, including a cryptographic fingerprint of the device certificate. This information is used for the remote-control security process and is stored locally.

5. Pairing PIN

A pairing PIN displayed by a compatible TV may be used to establish a secure pairing session. The App does not intentionally use the pairing PIN as a permanent account credential or transmit it to a separate cloud service.

6. Air Motion

If you enable Air Motion, the App can use the phone's supported motion sensors, such as the rotation sensor, to translate phone movement into remote-navigation commands.

Air Motion processing is performed locally on the phone. Sensor readings are not intentionally uploaded to an external server by the App.

7. Data Storage

The App may store information locally on the device that is necessary to remember paired devices and maintain secure connections. Security credentials are protected using Android's security facilities where applicable.

Local data may be removed by uninstalling the App or by using any applicable device/app controls for clearing the App's data.

8. Internet Access

The App does not require an Internet-based account or cloud service for normal TV remote operation. Network access used by the App is intended to support device discovery and communication.

9. Advertising and Analytics

The App is not designed to include advertising or third-party analytics for tracking user behavior.

10. Third-Party Services and SDKs

The App is designed to use Android platform security and networking facilities and libraries required for its remote-control functionality. We do not intentionally send the user's remote-control credentials or Air Motion sensor data to third-party services.

If a future version adds a third-party service that changes how personal or device data is collected, used, or shared, this Privacy Policy will be updated before that change is relied upon for the applicable functionality.

11. Children's Privacy

TV Remote is a utility application and does not intentionally collect personal information from children or adults. If you believe that personal information has been provided to the App in a manner inconsistent with this policy, please contact the developer using the contact method provided with the app's Google Play listing.

12. Data Sharing and Sale

We do not intentionally sell users' personal information. The App is designed so that normal remote-control communication occurs locally between the phone and the selected device rather than through a cloud service.

13. Security

We use reasonable technical measures appropriate to the App's purpose, including Android Keystore protection for the client private key and certificate-based authentication for supported secure connections.

No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Users should keep their Android device protected with the device security features they choose.

14. Your Choices

  • You can choose whether to grant requested local-network access.
  • You can choose whether to enable Air Motion.
  • You can stop using the App at any time.
  • You can clear the App's local data or uninstall the App to remove locally stored App data.

15. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App's functionality, data practices, or applicable requirements change. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when material changes are made.

16. Contact

For privacy questions or requests concerning TV Remote, please use the developer contact email shown on the App's Google Play Store listing.
